Minesweeper

Classic minesweeper, bafflingly refined

About the app

Baffled Cat Minesweeper is an Android game that brings the classic minesweeper experience to your phone with leaderboards, replay validation, and a touch of feline confusion.

Features

  • Classic minesweeper gameplay with multiple difficulty levels
  • Global leaderboards with nickname and country display
  • Replay validation and anti-cheat protection
  • Offline play with score sync when you're back online
