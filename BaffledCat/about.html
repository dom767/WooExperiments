About

A small studio, mildly confused

Baffled Cat is an independent studio building small games and audio-visual tools, one project at a time. No roadmap decks, no growth hacks — just things worth finishing.

What we make

Right now that's Minesweeper, a classic puzzle game rebuilt for Android with global leaderboards and a bit of anti-cheat spine, and Highpersonic 3, a browser-based audio visualiser that turns sound into generative geometry. More projects will land here as they're ready.

How we work

Everything ships small, gets used, and gets refined. If a feature doesn't earn its place, it doesn't ship. The cat approves of this policy, mostly by ignoring it and taking naps instead.
